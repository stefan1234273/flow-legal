Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Project FLOW handles information when you use FLOW Launcher and related services.

1. Information We May Receive

When you sign in using Discord, FLOW may receive the information permitted by the authorization screen, such as your Discord account identifier, username, and avatar information.

2. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to authenticate your account, display your profile, provide launcher features, and maintain the operation and security of FLOW.

3. Discord Authentication

Discord login is provided by Discord. Your Discord password is not requested by FLOW. Authentication takes place through Discord's authorization process.

Discord's privacy policy is available at discord.com/privacy.

4. Storage and Retention

The information stored by FLOW depends on the features you use and the version of the launcher. Before release, FLOW will document exactly what information is stored, where it is stored, and how long it is retained.

5. Sharing

FLOW does not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers needed to operate authentication and related features.

6. Security

We aim to use reasonable measures to protect information. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Your Choices

You can stop using FLOW and revoke its Discord authorization through your Discord account's authorized applications settings.

8. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when FLOW features or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the FLOW team through our official Discord community.
